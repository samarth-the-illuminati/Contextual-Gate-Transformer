Artificial intelligence is changing how students study and process documents. Long reports are difficult to read because important information is distributed across many pages. Offline summarization allows users to summarize private documents without sending data to a cloud server. Transformer models provide strong semantic representations for sentences. Mamba models can process long sequences efficiently using state-space modeling. A hybrid Transformer-Mamba system can combine semantic understanding with efficient document-level sequence processing. Redundancy-aware sentence selection can make the final summary shorter and more informative.
